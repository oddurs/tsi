Full rehearsal, fuelled: the features that give tsi its character.

tsi should show its work like a good teacher, know the real rockets people
argue about, and speak in missions rather than raw metres per second. This
milestone is where it stops being a calculator and becomes something people
enjoy opening.

**Exit criteria:** `tsi optimize ... --explain` walks through the derivation.
`tsi vehicle saturn-v --swap-engine raptor-2` answers a what-if. The engine
database cites a source for every number.
