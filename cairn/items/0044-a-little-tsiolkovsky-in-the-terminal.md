Small things that make tsi feel made by someone:
- `tsi --version` long form carries a Tsiolkovsky quote and the year of the rocket equation (1903)
- The man page gets a HISTORY section about Tsiolkovsky and *The Exploration of Cosmic Space by Means of Reaction Devices*
- `tsi calculate` with an impossible mass ratio notes that Tsiolkovsky's equation is logarithmic, and why that is the whole story of spaceflight
